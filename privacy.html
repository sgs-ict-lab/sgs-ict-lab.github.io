Student Privacy & Safety

What the classroom records

When enabled by the school, SGS ICT LAB Classroom stores the account email, name, username, display name, chosen profile picture, class and roll (if provided), completed learning checkpoints, submitted code and projects, teacher feedback, and support messages.

Who can access it?

Students see their own account and work. School-approved teachers and administrators can review students' profiles, progress, projects and school support messages. The public website does not show student records. Students cannot privately message other students through this feature.

Profile pictures

Choose one of six free built-in avatars, or optionally upload a photo. A photo can reveal your identity. Avoid uploading anyone else's photo without permission. Uploaded photos are stored in the school's Firebase Storage account; only registered students and authorized staff should be granted access through the required security rules.

For students and families

This proposed classroom is intended for supervised school learning. The school must approve its use, review parental consent and local policies for minors where required, decide how long student records are kept, and provide a clear process for students and families to request corrections or deletion. Do not submit passwords, home addresses, private documents, or sensitive information in messages or code.

Before launch

Accounts and databases are not active until the school's designated administrator configures Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Storage, and the supplied access rules. This page is a draft for school review and is not a substitute for the school's final privacy policy.
